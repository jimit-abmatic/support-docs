# Visitor Tracking Overview

**Turn anonymous website traffic into qualified pipeline.** Abmatic AI's visitor tracking identifies the companies and contacts visiting your website in real time — even when they never fill out a form. Stop guessing who is interested and start engaging prospects at the first sign of buying intent.

The whole experience lives under **Visitor Reveal** in the left sidebar (the magnifying-glass / TravelExplore icon). It has two tabs: **Accounts** and **Contacts**.

## Why Visitor Intelligence Matters

Most B2B websites convert only a small share of their visitors. Everyone else leaves anonymous — taking their buying intent with them. Visitor Reveal changes that by showing which companies and people are researching your solution.

| Without Visitor Tracking | With Abmatic Visitor Reveal |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Most visitors stay anonymous | Identify companies behind their visits |
| Miss buying-intent signals | See which accounts are actively researching |
| React only to form fills | Engage prospects at the first sign of interest |
| No context for sales outreach | Rich company and contact data for personalization |
| Blind spots in your funnel | Clear visibility into website engagement |

**The result:** sales focuses on accounts already showing interest, marketing personalizes in real time, and pipeline grows faster.

## How De-Anonymization Works (In Simple Terms)

When someone visits your website, they are anonymous to you — but not to Abmatic AI. Here is how unknown visitors become actionable records.

### Step 1: Company Identification (IP-Based)

Every device on the internet has an IP address, and many companies use dedicated IP ranges that map back to them. When a visitor lands on your site, Abmatic AI matches their IP address against a large company database to identify the organization behind the visit.

**What this means for you:** even if a visitor never fills out a form, you can see that "someone from a company visited your pricing page three times this week."

### Step 2: Contact Matching

Abmatic AI goes beyond company-level data to identify the specific people on your site:

| Method | How It Works |
|--------|-------------|
| **Form submissions** | A visitor fills out any form on your site, which links their email, name, and company to all past and future visits |
| **Email link clicks** | A visitor clicks a link in your marketing email; the click-through URL identifies them and connects to their browsing session |
| **Contact database matching** | IP identification is combined with professional contact data to surface known contacts who match the visitor's company and role |

### Step 3: Enrichment and Scoring

Once a visitor is identified, Abmatic AI enriches the record with firmographic data (industry, revenue, employee count) and calculates an engagement score based on visit frequency, pages viewed, and depth of engagement.

### Understanding Reveal Confidence

Not every identification is equally certain. Abmatic AI assigns a **Reveal Confidence** bucket so you know how much to trust each match. Each visitor gets a numeric confidence value that maps to one of six labels:

| Reveal Confidence | Score Range | What It Means | Your Best Move |
|-------------------|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Very High** | 71–100 | Direct match from a dedicated corporate IP range | Prioritize for immediate sales outreach |
| **High** | 51–70 | Strong signals with high certainty | Add to target account lists and sequences |
| **Moderate** | 31–50 | Good match, some uncertainty | Include in personalization and ad campaigns |
| **Low** | 21–30 | Possible match, worth monitoring | Use for awareness; verify before direct outreach |
| **Very Low** | 6–20 | Weak signal | Monitor for repeat visits that increase confidence |
| **None** | 0–5 | No reliable signal yet | Wait for more activity |

In the grid, confidence renders as a colored chip (green for high buckets, amber for the middle, red for the lowest), and the tiles across the top of the page double as one-click filters.

:::tip Focus Where It Counts
Start your outreach with Very High and High confidence matches. Use Moderate matches in advertising campaigns where broader reach is acceptable and false positives cost less.
:::

## The Grid: Filtering, Sorting, and Bulk Actions

Both Reveal tabs render the same powerful server-side grid (the same one used across All Accounts and All Contacts). Because data is fetched from the server as you scroll, the grid handles very large lists smoothly.

- **Search** by company name, domain, or keyword.
- **FILTERS** opens column- and field-level filters (industry, revenue, confidence, last seen, pages visited, and more). Your filter and column choices are saved and reapplied next time you open the page.
- **Sort** any column — including **Reveal Last Seen** to surface the most recent visitors.
- **Infinite scroll** loads more rows as you reach the bottom, and a live count shows how many rows match.
- **Select rows** with the checkboxes to run bulk actions. You can select everything that matches your current filter, or use **select-all-except** to deselect a few rows from a full-list selection.

:::note Server-side grid
The Accounts and Contacts grids now load data from the server (server-side row model). Filtering, sorting, and counts all happen against the full dataset, not just the rows currently on screen — so a filter applies to every matching company, even ones not yet scrolled into view.
:::

## Concrete Use Cases

### "See which companies are visiting your pricing page right now"

Your pricing page is one of the strongest buying-intent signals on your site. Filter the Accounts tab to show only companies that visited `/pricing`, sorted by Reveal Confidence and recency. Your sales team gets a ready-to-work list of accounts actively evaluating you.

### "Identify the actual people behind anonymous website visits"

A company visit is useful, but knowing the specific person — with a verified work email, job title, and engagement score — is transformational. The Contacts tab connects anonymous sessions to real people.

### "Build a target list from your most engaged visitors"

Filter by engagement score, confidence, and industry to build a list of your best-fit, most-engaged visitors. Export it, sync it to your CRM, or use it to power personalized website experiences.

### "Alert your sales team when a target account visits your site"

Connect Slack so reps get an instant alert when a named account visits high-intent pages. No more delayed follow-ups.

## Best Practices for Acting on Reveal Data

### Speed Matters

Reveal data decays fast. A company that visited your pricing page today is far more likely to respond than one that visited two weeks ago. Build a daily review habit:

| When | What to Do | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| **Every morning** | Check Very High and High confidence visitors from the past 24 hours | Catch active buyers while intent is fresh |
| **Before sales calls** | Look up the prospect's account in Visitor Reveal | Walk in with full context on what they researched |
| **Weekly** | Review engagement trends and build new target lists | Spot opportunities before competitors do |

### Quality Over Quantity

Use the data to be relevant, not loud:

- **Reference their behavior:** "I noticed your team has been researching [topic]."
- **Match the contact to the message:** a VP of Engineering and a Director of Marketing need different pitches.
- **Respect confidence:** outreach to a Very High match is informed; outreach to a Very Low match is a guess.
