# Navigating Abmatic AI

Once you log in, every page in Abmatic AI shares the same layout: a slim **icon sidebar** on the left for moving between sections, and a **top bar** on the right for search, help, notifications, dark mode, and settings. This page is your map to both.

![Abmatic AI Home with the left icon sidebar and top bar](/img/screenshots/nav-sidebar-collapsed.png)

---

## The Left Icon Sidebar

The sidebar is the main way you move around Abmatic AI. It sits on the far left of every screen.

- **Collapsed by default.** It shows just icons in a narrow rail (about 54px wide) so it stays out of your way.
- **Expands on hover.** Move your mouse over the rail and it slides open to about 200px, revealing the text label next to each icon. Move your mouse away and it collapses again.
- **Selected item is highlighted.** Whatever section you are currently in shows a rounded pill behind its icon so you can see where you are at a glance.
- **The logo goes Home.** The Abmatic AI logo and **ABMATIC AI** wordmark sit at the top. Click the logo at any time to return to the Home page.

![Sidebar expanded on hover, showing the label next to each icon](/img/screenshots/nav-sidebar-expanded.png)

*Hover over the rail to reveal labels for every section.*

### Sidebar Items (top to bottom)

| Icon | Section | Where It Takes You |
|------|---------|--------------------|
| ✨ | **AI Agent** | The in-app AI assistant (`/ai-agents/abm`) |
| 🚩 | **Goals** | Oracle Goals (`/oracle/goals`) — *only on accounts with Oracle enabled* |
| 🏠 | **Home** | Your Home dashboard (`/`) |
| 💼 | **Accounts** | All accounts and account collections (`/accounts`) |
| 👥 | **Contacts** | All contacts and contact collections (`/contacts`) |
| 📣 | **Campaigns** | Create and manage campaigns (`/campaigns`) |
| 🪪 | **Conversions** | Conversion goals and tracking (`/conversions`) |
| 🧭 | **Visitor Reveal** | Identified companies and contacts (`/reveal`) |
| 📈 | **Analytics** | Reports and dashboards (`/analytics`) |
| 📥 | **Oracle Inbox** | Approve or deny Oracle actions (`/oracle/inbox`) — *only on accounts with Oracle enabled* |

At the very **bottom** of the rail sits one more item:

| Icon | Section | Where It Takes You |
|------|---------|--------------------|
| `</>` | **Installation** | Get and verify your tracking script (`/installation`) |

:::info Some items are turned on per account
**Goals** and **Oracle Inbox** are part of Abmatic AI's autonomous-agent product (Oracle) and only appear when your account has the feature enabled. If you do not see them, that is expected. To explore Oracle, contact your Abmatic AI representative or email [support@abmatic.ai](mailto:support@abmatic.ai).
:::

## The Top Bar

The top bar runs across the top of every page. On the **left** it shows the current page title or breadcrumb (for example, "Home" or "Home / Home Dashboard"). On the **right** is a toolbar of quick actions.

![The top-right toolbar: dark mode, search, help, notifications, and settings](/img/screenshots/nav-topbar.png)

From left to right, the top-right toolbar contains:

| Button | What It Does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Dark / Light mode** | Switches the whole app between light and dark themes |
| **Search** | Opens global search across your data |
| **Help** | Opens a support request form |
| **Notifications** | A bell with an unread-count badge for in-app alerts |
| **Settings / account** | Opens your account settings (`/settings/account`) |

## Global Search

Click the **Search** (magnifying glass) icon to open the search panel. It anchors to the top-right of the screen and stays out of your way while you type.

![Global search panel showing mixed results across accounts, contacts, campaigns, and more](/img/screenshots/nav-global-search.png)

*Type in the "What are you looking for?" field and results appear across every object type.*

- The field is labeled **Search** with the placeholder **"What are you looking for?"**
- Results appear as you type (there is a brief debounce so it waits until you pause).
- Search looks across **six** kinds of records, each with its own icon and a direct link to that record:

| Result Type | Opens |
|-------------|-------|
| **Account** | The account's overview page |
| **Contact** | The contact's overview page |
| **Campaign** | The campaign editor |
| **Report** | The report detail page |
| **Dashboard** | The dashboard detail page |
| **Collection** | The collection — the link adapts to the collection's status |

For **collections**, the link is status-aware: a draft collection opens its filter builder, a previewing collection opens its preview, and a finished collection opens its records table. Each collection result also shows a small status chip (Draft, Preview, Processing, Done, or Failed).

If nothing matches, you will see a simple **"No Results"** message.

## Help and Support

Click the **Help** (question mark) icon to open the **Help** dialog. Type your question (at least a few characters) and click **Send**.

> "Write your query for troubleshooting and we will get back to you within 24 hours."

After you send, Abmatic AI confirms it received your message and the team will reply by email **within 24 hours**.

---

## Notifications

The **bell** icon is your in-app notification feed. A badge on the bell shows how many unread notifications you have. Click it to open the feed and read or clear your alerts.

---

## Dark Mode

Use the **dark / light mode toggle** (sun or moon icon) in the top bar to switch themes. The button tooltip reads **"Switch to Dark Mode"** or **"Switch to Light Mode"** depending on your current theme.

![Home page shown in dark mode](/img/screenshots/nav-dark-mode.png)

*The same Home page in dark mode — every screen supports both themes.*

- The app defaults to **light mode**.
- Your choice is remembered in your browser, so Abmatic AI opens in whichever mode you last used.
- Every page supports both themes, so feel free to use whichever is easier on your eyes.

---

## The AI Agent Drawer

The top sidebar item, **AI Agent**, opens Abmatic AI's in-app assistant. On many pages you can also pop open a slide-in **"AI Conversation"** panel on the right side of the screen, which lets you ask the agent questions without leaving the page you are on. The main content shifts left to make room, and closing the panel returns you to the page as it was.

:::tip Find your way fast
- **Lost?** Click the Abmatic AI logo to jump back Home.
- **Looking for a specific account, contact, campaign, or report?** Use global search instead of clicking through menus.
- **Need a hand?** The Help button reaches our team — replies come within 24 hours.
:::
