In this short walkthrough, we'll create your first campaign in Abmatic AI — from picking a type to tracking results.

Welcome to Abmatic AI. This is your home dashboard. To build a campaign, head to Campaigns in the left navigation.

This is the Campaigns page — every personalized experience, ad, and AI sequence lives here. To start a new one, click the Campaign button in the top right.

Abmatic groups campaigns into three families: Personalization, Advertising, and Agentic — the AI-driven ones. For your first campaign, choose Inbound, which personalizes what visitors see on your own website.

Once created, you land in the campaign. Here you set who the campaign targets, design the personalized experience, and preview exactly what each visitor will see.

After you launch, the Insights tab shows performance as visitors engage — unique visitors, conversions, and how personalization is lifting your results. And that's your first campaign.

## Build your audiences (Accounts & Contacts)

<div style={{position:'relative',paddingBottom:'56.25%',height:0,borderRadius:'12px',overflow:'hidden',margin:'1.5rem 0'}}>
  <video controls preload="metadata" style={{position:'absolute',top:0,left:0,width:'100%',height:'100%'}} src="https://cdn.abmatic.ai/training/05-audiences.mp4"></video>
</div>

🖱️ Prefer to click through it yourself? **[Open the interactive walkthrough →](https://cdn.abmatic.ai/training/05-audiences/interactive/index.html)**

<details>
  <summary>Transcript</summary>

Every campaign needs an audience. In Abmatic AI, those are your Accounts and Contacts. Let's see how to organize them.

Accounts are the companies you want to reach. The Collections tab groups them into reusable lists — by industry, intent, renewal date, anything you like.

To build a new list, use the Account List button. You can create one from filters, upload a CSV, or pull it straight from your CRM.

Contacts work exactly the same way, but for individual people — so you can target the right buyers inside each account.

That's audiences. Build the right list once, and you can point any campaign at it.

</details>
